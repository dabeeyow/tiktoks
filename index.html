Faceless Reels

A personal tool for scheduling and publishing short videos to my own social accounts.

What it does

Faceless Reels is a personal automation tool that generates short vertical videos (background footage, a voiceover, and captions) and publishes them on a daily schedule to the owner's own connected TikTok and YouTube accounts.

How it uses TikTok

  • The account owner signs in once with TikTok Login to authorize the tool.
  • The tool uploads a finished video and publishes it to the owner's own account using the Content Posting API (Direct Post).
  • It never posts to, reads, or accesses any account other than the owner's own.
